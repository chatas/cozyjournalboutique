Privacy & Cookies

The short version

This website is a simple catalogue that links to our Etsy shop. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking cookies here, and we do not collect your name, email or payment details on this site. All purchases and downloads happen securely on Etsy.

What we store on your device

The only thing we save is a small “essential” entry in your browser's local storage to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice, so it doesn't keep reappearing. It contains no personal information and is never shared.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — we load fonts from Google's servers so the site looks the way it should. Your browser contacts Google to fetch them; see Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Etsy — product images are shown from Etsy's image servers, and the “Shop on Etsy” buttons take you to Etsy. Once you're on Etsy, Etsy's own privacy & cookie policy applies, including any cookies Etsy sets.
  • Pinterest — if you arrive from Pinterest, Pinterest may use its own cookies on their platform. We don't place a Pinterest tag on this site.

Your choices

Because we don't use tracking cookies, there's nothing to opt out of here. You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to reset the cookie notice.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Message us any time through our Etsy shop.

If we ever add analytics or advertising in the future, we'll update this page and ask for consent where required.
